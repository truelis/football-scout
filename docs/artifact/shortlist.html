What the model can and cannot see

Where the list comes from

The €4M cap relocates the shortlist to tier 2 and 3. That is the price filter working, not a fault — but it means the top five leagues supply very few names.

Only 23 of 166 are scored on xG

Understat covers the top five leagues plus Russia and nothing else. Everyone outside it is ranked on goal contributions, a coarser proxy.

  • The two are percentiled separately. Ranking npxG+xA against goal contributions in one pool would put two different metrics on one scale and call the result a percentile.
  • Transfermarkt→Understat entity resolution runs at 97.5% coverage across the top five, enforced as a build failure below 90%.
  • Finishing vs npxG is shown but never scored — it is noise over a single season.

Known blind spots

Stated plainly, because a confident number over a thin metric is worse than no number.

  • No injury, disciplinary or off-field data. A name near the top can be unsignable for reasons the model cannot see.
  • League coefficients are placeholders. Club Elo's API has been down (HTTP 502), so league strength still runs on hand-written guesses — and they carry much of the ranking.
  • Defenders and goalkeepers are excluded, not ranked badly. There are no defensive metrics in the free data.
  • Thin samples flatter some players. Minutes are shown on every row for that reason.
Built from transfermarkt-datasets (market values, appearances, contracts) and Understat (xG, xA, xGChain). Market value is not a transfer fee — estimated fee applies a 1.8× multiplier, and fees for prospects run 1.5–2.5× above Transfermarkt value. This is a shortlist to investigate, not a verdict.
